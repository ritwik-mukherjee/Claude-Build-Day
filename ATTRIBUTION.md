# Third-party assets

## 3D models (CC BY 4.0) — `public/assets/models/`, arena sprites in `public/assets/sprites/hulk/`
- “HULK RIGGED WITH ANIMATION” by Kapider22 — https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hulk-rigged-with-animation-70a9e594410147b595e9ac434afa7d44 — CC BY 4.0. Arena ragdoll part sprites are rendered from this model by `scripts/bake/parts.ts` (adapted: segmented, re-lit, downscaled).
- “God of War 3 Kratos - Rigged and Texture” by S4int0fSSG — https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/god-of-war-3-kratos-rigged-and-texture-ad37e8d6a2e44f8691bf2f2ab5bdbfa9 — CC BY 4.0.
- “Doraemon 3D” by arc98tect — https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/doraemon-3d-c2256852d3b6443d867eee85e1588ff2 — CC BY 4.0.

Textures were downscaled to ≤1024 px by `scripts/prepare_assets.py`. Each model folder keeps its original `license.txt`.

**Caveat:** these licences cover the model files only. They do not grant rights to the underlying characters (Hulk © Marvel, Kratos © Sony Interactive Entertainment, Doraemon © Fujiko-Pro), which remain the property of their owners. This is a non-commercial demo.
